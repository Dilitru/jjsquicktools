# jjsquicktools
JJ's Quick Tools for After Effects

## What's New?

- **AUTO STROKE TRACE:** Uses AI. Animates handwriting by automating the stroke order.
- **EDGE LIGHT SWEEP:** Makes an edge light move from left to right.
- **SMAC STEPS TEXT:** Colorful and playful text with violator.
- **Trim Empty Audio At Start:** Cuts out the silent part at the start of audio clips.

---

- **SPIN IN:** The layer spins into appearance
- **PULSING LOOP:** The layer grows then shrinks in a loop
- **ECHO TEXT:** 80s Style Text in. Laser Text enters
- **GLITCH TRANSITION:** Quick glitchy transition. Adjustable.
- **SLOW LIGHT SWEEP** 
- **RAINBOW REFRACTION:** Add sparkling rainbow effects
- **CHROMATIC ABBERATION:** Makes selected comp have chromatic abberation
- **FILM GRUNGE:** Film grunge effect
- **BOKEH BACKGROUND:** giant bokeh circles adjustable

---

- **AUTO-TRIM:** There is now an auto-trim button. All Animate In and Animate Out will automatically trim the in and out points. Press the button to toggle this behaviour off (just like in earlier versions).
- **ANIME SPEED LINES:** Creates a comp that has speed lines. The hole in the middle is adjustable.
- **GAME WIN TEXT:** Recreates the "GAME WIN!" graphic ticker from the Pokemon championships. Editable.
- **LOOP OUT:** Automatically creates proper loop out expressions. 4 options.
